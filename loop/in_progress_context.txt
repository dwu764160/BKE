2026-07-17 — PEC Step 3 architectural overhaul: scale decompression + talent dominance
- Critical issue: model produced compressed net ratings (std=0.64 vs real NBA ~5.5)
  with zero predictive correlation (r=0.04) and structure/defense dominating talent.
- Root causes identified and fixed:
  1. Rim protector detection: was str.contains("rim") catching only 40/130 bigs.
     Fixed to include "Dropping Big"(60) and "Mobile Big"(30) via .isin().
     Result: teams_no_rp dropped from 60% to 4.3%.
  2. DEFAULT_TEAM_SCALE: increased from 5 to 20.
     BKE compression factor ~9x vs real RAPM (std 0.34 vs 3.0).
     TEAM_SCALE=20 produces realistic NBA spread (std~5.07, range -11 to +13).
  3. Data-driven TEAM_SCALE threshold: raised from r>0.05 to r>0.20.
     With corrupted data (overall r=0.074), data-driven fit was degenerate.
     Now falls back to DEFAULT_TEAM_SCALE when talent-actual r is weak.
  4. All penalty/bonus magnitudes reduced 50-70% via named constants:
     PLAYMAKING_SOLO: -0.7 to -0.25, SPACING_POOR: -1.0 to -0.35,
     RP_MISSING: -1.2 to -0.40, POA_MISSING: -0.8 to -0.25, etc.
  5. Volatility model enhanced: added roster depth component,
     wider alpha coefficients (1 to 5 for 3PA/creation, 0.5 to 3 for transition),
     floor/ceiling reduced from [8,16] to [1,6].
  6. Calibration skip logic: when holdout_r < 0 (inverted data) or
     train_r < 0.20 or slope < 0.10, skip OLS calibration and use
     raw predictions with mean centering. This preserves realistic spread.
  7. All constants centralized with documentation in constants.py.
